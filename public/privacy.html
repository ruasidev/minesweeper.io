Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

In short: Minesweeper World keeps what it needs to run your account and the leaderboards, and nothing else. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and your information is never sold.

Who runs this

Minesweeper World (minesweeper.wooasi.com) is a hobby project run by an individual developer, referred to here as "we". For anything about your information, write to support@wooasi.com.

What we collect

If you play without an account, we keep nothing about you. Your settings and theme are kept in your own browser.

If you make an account, we keep:

  • Your email address, your display name and your username.
  • Your password, only in a scrambled (hashed) form. We cannot read it.
  • A profile photo and a bio, if you add them.
  • How you play: the games you finish (board, time, result), your daily challenge attempts, multiplayer results, experience points and level, rating, badges, how many games you have started and how long you have spent in them.
  • A record of each game you play while signed in: the board and every move you made on it, with its timing. This is what lets results be trusted and cheating be found.
  • For each device you are signed in on, its IP address and browser type. This is used to keep sign-in secure.

Chat messages (in a room or in the global chat) are shown to the other players there, with your username. The server holds only the most recent 50 messages of each chat in memory. They are not saved to our database, and they are gone when the room closes or the server restarts.

Signing in with Google

If you choose "Continue with Google", Google tells us your name, your email address, your profile picture and an identifier for your Google account, and gives us sign-in tokens that cover only that basic profile. We use this only to create your account and sign you in. We do not get access to your email, contacts, files or anything else in your Google account, and we do not pass what Google gives us to anyone else.

You can remove Minesweeper World's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Cookies and browser storage

  • One cookie keeps you signed in. Another exists briefly while you sign in with Google. Both are needed for sign-in to work.
  • Your settings (theme, sound, layout) are saved in your browser's local storage and stay on your device.
  • There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

To run the game: signing you in, showing your profile, keeping leaderboards, running multiplayer games, and stopping cheating and abuse. That includes checking game records for play that no person could manage, taking such results off the leaderboards, and banning accounts that cheat or abuse other players. We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

What other people can see

  • Anyone: your username, profile photo and best times, where they appear on a leaderboard.
  • Other signed-in players: your profile, which is your display name, username, photo, bio, the date you joined, your level, badges and playing statistics.
  • Other signed-in players, too: your match history, which is the list of games you have played while signed in, and a replay of each, move by move. A go at the daily challenge is only shown to others once that day is over.
  • Players in the same chat: what you write there.
  • Nobody else sees your email address. The people who administer the site can see it.

If you signed up with Google, your display name starts out as your Google name. You can change it in your profile.

Who we share it with

Only the services that the site runs on:

  • Railway hosts our server and database, where account and play information is stored.
  • GitHub Pages serves the website itself.
  • Google, only if you sign in with Google.
  • Resend delivers the emails we send you, such as the one asking you to confirm your address. It is given your email address and name to do that.

Like any web host, Railway and GitHub see the IP address of everyone who visits, signed in or not. These services may store and process information in the United States or other countries, which may be outside the country you live in. Beyond them, we share information only if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Account and play information is kept for as long as your account exists. Sign-in sessions expire by themselves after a period without use. Chat messages are not stored.

Your choices

  • You can change your display name, username, photo and bio in your profile at any time.
  • You can ask for a copy of your information, or for your account and everything attached to it to be deleted, by writing to support@wooasi.com from the email address of the account. We will do it within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live, the law may give you further rights over your information, such as correcting it or objecting to how it is used. Write to the same address and we will help.

Children

Accounts are not for children under 13, or under the age at which your country lets you agree to this on your own. If we learn that such an account exists, we will delete it.

Security

Everything travels over an encrypted connection and passwords are stored hashed. No website can promise perfect security, and this one is in beta, so please use a password you do not use anywhere else.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date at the top.
